Model paragraph: “Social media is harmful to teenagers”

Against the use of social media by teenagers

First of all, it is widely believed that social media has a damaging effect on teenagers’ mental health. By this I mean that constant exposure to carefully curated images encourages young people to compare themselves unfavourably with others. To illustrate this point, a recent study found that teenagers who spend more than three hours a day on social platforms report significantly higher levels of anxiety and low self-esteem. This clearly demonstrates that the more time adolescents spend online, the greater the risk to their emotional wellbeing. Therefore, it is reasonable to conclude that unrestricted social media use is a serious threat to the mental health of young people.
